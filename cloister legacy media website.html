Why Cloister

Cloister Legacy Media is a video production company focused on one thing: preserving and sharing stories. We want to tell your stories.

From the first conversation to final delivery you will work with our documentary filmmaker Sean Mowry. His short documentary "All the Queens Houses" premiered at DOC NYC, the largest documentary festival in the country. It then premiered as a Vimeo Staff Pick and has earned nearly 100k views. He has worked in post production on multiple Frontline specials for PBS and developed documentary series for HBO and Discovery+.
